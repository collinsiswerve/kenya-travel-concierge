PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES

A little clarity.
Before you connect.

Making an enquiry

The website enquiry form prepares a message on your device. It does not submit your details to a KTC website database. You choose whether to send your prepared message through WhatsApp or your email application.

The details you share

We ask for your name, email, preferred service, and a short description so we can understand your request. Your phone number and timing are optional. Please do not include passport numbers, payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive documents in an initial enquiry.

External services

Links to WhatsApp and Instagram open services operated by their respective providers, which apply their own privacy practices. The website uses no advertising pixels, marketing cookies, or enquiry analytics scripts. Hosting services may process technical information needed to serve the website.

Following up with KTC

When you send a message, KTC uses the information you provide to discuss and coordinate your requested services. Any further documents, third-party arrangements, or authorisations should be agreed directly with your concierge.

Contact

For questions about an enquiry or the information you have shared, contact info.kenyatravelconcierge@gmail.com or call +254 717 762323.
